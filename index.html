CREONIX is the official social media and creative club of our college — a crew of photographers, filmmakers, designers and writers who make campus life impossible to scroll past.

From the first freshers’ welcome to the last convocation cheer, we document, design and share the moments that define our college — and we build the skills of every member along the way.
